The goal of the program to find out whether intelligence is an inevitable outcome for evolution using a simulation of living things
This simulation will be a 2d simulation with basic objects, an animals  and a day night cycle
animals are things that have HP and food bar, once their HP gets to 0 they die, and if their food bar is over a certain level and in range of another thing of the same species they reproduce ie. spawn in a new animal

only ground animals, no areal or aquatic animals

the simulation will simulate different species, species will have a collections of traits 
species trait may have category, species only one trait from each category
the species itself will not change during the simulation

the animals will interact with various sim object

the simulation will simulate in 2d and be rendered in 3d

tech stack: kotlin (backend) which is than transpiled into WebAssembly, three.js/react/typescript (frontend),

follow for instructions on specific topic /docs

Terrain are generated using basic perlin-noise with a tile system with the following terrain type FLESH WATER, PLAIN, FOREST AND DESERT
animal would only be able to walk on ground only, 

project visual style: low poly, colorful, family friendly, cute. all visuals should be generated locally, no external models will be used
